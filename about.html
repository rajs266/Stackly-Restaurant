📖 Our Story

How Stackly Came To Life

One Hungry Night Changed Everything 🌙

Back in 2012, our founder Arjun was wandering Salem's streets after midnight, stomach growling, desperately searching for food that wasn't just "edible" but actually exciting.

That one grumbling stomach sparked an idea: "Why can't great food be FUN?" And just like that, Stackly was born — not just as a restaurant, but as a celebration of flavour, laughter, and zero boring bites!

Today, we're Salem's happiest dining spot. From crispy starters to decadent desserts, we stack greatness on every single plate. Our kitchen is loud, our flavours are bold, and our vibes are absolutely unmatched! 🔥

⏰ Journey

Our Timeline

From a tiny kitchen to Salem's #1 — here's how we stacked up!

2012 🚀
Stackly opens its doors with just 4 tables, 2 chefs, and a dream to make food fun again!
2015 🏆
Won "Best New Restaurant" in Salem. The secret? Our legendary BBQ sauce recipe!
2018 🎉
Expanded to 80 seats, added live music nights, and introduced our famous "Chaos Pizza"!
2022 ⭐
Named "Best Family Restaurant" three years running. Our community = our family!
2025 🔥
12 years of slapping flavours! 5000+ happy customers and counting. The journey continues!
